Vidrom Assistant

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Vidrom Assistant handles messages sent through WhatsApp and related service data.

1. Who operates Vidrom Assistant

Vidrom Assistant is operated by Vidrom. In this policy, “Vidrom,” “we,” and “us” refer to the operator of the assistant and its supporting services.

2. Information we receive

When you contact Vidrom Assistant through WhatsApp, we may receive and process:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number, WhatsApp display name, and message metadata provided by Meta.
  • The text, attachments, or other content that you send to the assistant.
  • The assistant’s response, delivery status, timestamps, and technical error information.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information unless it is necessary for your request.

3. How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Receive and respond to your WhatsApp questions.
  • Find relevant passages in business documents made available to Vidrom Assistant and use them to prepare document-grounded answers.
  • Maintain message processing status, prevent duplicate processing, troubleshoot failures, and protect the service.
  • Improve the reliability, security, and administration of the assistant.

4. Business documents and generated answers

Vidrom Assistant may use business documents supplied by the business operating the assistant to answer questions. Those documents are not treated as public content merely because they are used by the assistant.

Answers are generated from the available documents and may be incomplete or inaccurate. Do not rely on an answer as a substitute for professional, emergency, legal, medical, or financial advice.

5. Service providers

We use service providers to operate the assistant, including:

  • Meta, which provides the WhatsApp Business Platform and delivers messages.
  • Amazon Web Services, which provides hosting, storage, message processing, logging, and security services.
  • Amazon Bedrock, which processes relevant message and document text to generate an answer.

These providers process information only as needed to provide, secure, and maintain the service, subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Retention and security

We retain message and technical records only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the assistant, maintain security, resolve operational issues, meet legal obligations, and enforce our agreements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of record and the business using the assistant.

We use access controls, encryption, and other reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information we process. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and deletion requests

You may stop using the assistant at any time. To request deletion of personal information associated with your WhatsApp interaction, contact vidromdv@gmail.com and include the WhatsApp phone number used to contact the assistant and enough detail to identify the relevant conversation.

We may need to verify the request before acting on it. We may retain information where required by law, needed to resolve disputes, or necessary to protect the security of the service.

8. Children

Vidrom Assistant is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the assistant, its providers, or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.

Effective date: September 12, 2026

10. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact vidromdv@gmail.com.
